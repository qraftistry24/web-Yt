QRAFTISTRY WEBSITE

Live website: https://qraftistry24.github.io/web-Yt/

Publish index.html, assets/, services/, robots.txt and sitemap.xml together at the repository root. Do not nest them inside another site/ folder.

The new founder photo is assets/yash-trivedi.png, exactly as supplied.

Optional integrations in assets/site.js:
- Google Analytics: replace G-XXXXXXXXXX with your own measurement ID. Tracking stays disabled until configured.
- Web3Forms: replace YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY with a key for your own inbox. Until configured, the inquiry form opens a prepared WhatsApp message and offers email; the visitor must press Send.
- BEFORE_AFTER stays empty/hidden until genuine matching pairs are supplied.

Shared CSS and JavaScript URLs have a version parameter to refresh browser caches.
