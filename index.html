How we help brands in practice.

Amazon.in product page and creatives

Dau Gopal Sabudana

Challenge
Sabudana looks the same on every shelf, so shoppers have little reason to pick one pack over another.
What we did
Designed a side-by-side comparison creative showing pearl quality, size and packaging, plus a usage creative for the brand's Chat Masala.
Outcome
A consistent set of on-brand creatives for the brand's Amazon presence, with the product page live on Amazon.in.

Amazon brand storefront

Viaggio Espresso

Challenge
A range of coffee-capsule flavours needed one branded place where shoppers could browse and compare.
What we did
Set up a storefront with Home, Coffee Pods, Best Seller and Flavours Assortment pages, plus an intensity guide for each flavour.
Outcome
A branded storefront live on Amazon.in with every flavour, bundle and multipack in one place.

Brand creatives

Caffè Cagliari

Challenge
An Italian coffee brand needed its story and capsule details to look premium and consistent online.
What we did
Designed a brand-story creative and a capsule creative with intensity, body, aroma and sweetness ratings.
Outcome
A matching creative set that presents the brand's heritage and product details clearly.
